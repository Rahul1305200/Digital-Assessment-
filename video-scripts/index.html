  1. Sign in

    • Use your own team login.

    This is the dashboard of the digital assessment system, where Derek and the team sign in.

  2. New patients appear

    • Patients show up as soon as they scan.
    • Then they answer 2 questions.
    • Each row shows a study code and email.

    By scanning the QR code, patients appear here. Then they answer two questions. Each one gets a study code, not a name.

  3. Press Start session

    • Patient is ready → press Start session.
    • Randomly assigned: YouMind care or usual care.
    • Groups always stay 50/50.

    When a patient is ready, the team presses Start session. The system divides all participants into two equal groups, and randomly assigns each one to YouMind care or usual care.

  4. Session running

    • YouMind care listens on headphones.
    • Usual care waits.
    • The top button shows how many are listening.

    Now the session is running. The YouMind care group listens, and the usual care group waits.

  5. Doctor ready: Stop all music

    • The doctor arrives for the procedure.
    • Press Stop all music.
    • The music stops for everyone, and it’s recorded.

    When the doctor arrives for the procedure, the team presses Stop all music. The music stops for everyone at once, and the time it stopped is recorded.

  6. Press Open T2 for all

    • Just before the procedure.
    • Everyone answers the same 2 questions again.
    • YouMind care also says how the listening went.

    Just before the procedure, the team presses Open T2 for all. Every patient answers the same two questions again, so we can see how they feel now. The YouMind care group also says how the listening went.

  7. Fill the Study record

    • Open the patient → Study record.
    • Add the timing, any delays and safety concerns.
    • Press Save.

    During the procedure, the team fills in the study record for each patient. It records the timing, any delays and any safety concerns. Then they press Save.

  8. Close assessment (only if needed)

    • Only if the questions aren’t finished.
    • Press Close assessment and pick a reason.
    • Every missing answer is explained.

    If the procedure starts before a patient finishes their questions, the team presses Close assessment and picks a reason. That way, every missing answer is explained.

  9. Press Open evening for all

    • That evening.
    • Every patient gets a short check-in.
    • Same link as before.

    That evening, the team presses Open evening for all. Every patient gets a short check-in on the same link.

  10. Press Open sleep for all

    • The next morning.
    • Only patients who agreed get it.

    The next morning, the team presses Open sleep for all. Patients who agreed get a short sleep survey.

  11. Patient page

    • Click any patient.
    • All their answers, step by step.
    • YouMind care: when they played, paused, skipped or stopped the music.

    The team can click any patient to see all their answers. For YouMind care, it also shows how they listened: when they played, paused, skipped or stopped the music.

  12. Press Export CSV

    • One file, one row per patient.
    • Emails are never included.

    Finally, the team presses Export CSV. All the study data downloads in one file, one row per patient, without emails.
