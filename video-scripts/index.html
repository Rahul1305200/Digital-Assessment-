  2. 3–8sPatients appear in the list

    • Every patient who scanned, consented and answered T0 appears live.
    • Each row shows the study code, the email (click to copy) and “waiting for the team”.

  3. 8–13sPress Start session

    • Press Start session on the patient’s row.
    • The system picks music or no music, keeping the groups 50/50.
    • The patient’s phone moves on by itself.

    When the patient is ready, Derek presses Start. The system puts them in the music or no-music group, always keeping the two groups fifty-fifty.

  4. 13–17sSessions running

    • Each row shows the group and “Session in progress”.
    • Stop all music (1) shows how many are listening right now.

    The music group listens on headphones, the others wait as usual. Derek can see who is listening at any moment.

  5. 17–21sDoctor ready: Stop all music

    • Press Stop all music → Stop the music now.
    • Every music player turns grey and stops within seconds.
    • The stop is recorded on each patient’s timeline.

    If the doctor is ready, one tap on Stop all music stops every player at once, and the stop is recorded.

  6. 21–25sPress Open T2 for all

    • Opens the before-procedure questions for everyone waiting.
    • The music group also answers how the listening went.

    Just before the procedure, Open T2 for all sends the same two questions again. The music group also says how the listening went.

  7. 25–30sFill the Study record → Save

    • Open the patient → Study record.
    • Procedure, consent, timing, delays, interruptions, safety concerns.

    During the procedure, Derek fills in the study record: the procedure, timing, any delay, interruption or safety concern.

  8. 30–33sClose assessment (only if needed)

    • If the procedure starts before a questionnaire is finished.
    • Pick the reason, e.g. “Physician ready to proceed”.

    If the procedure starts before a questionnaire is finished, Close assessment records the reason, so nothing is left unexplained.

  9. 33–36sPress Open evening for all

    • That evening, opens the follow-up for every patient at once.

    That evening, one click opens the evening follow-up for every patient.

  10. 36–39sPress Open sleep for all

    • Next morning, opens the optional sleep survey for those who agreed.

    Next morning, one click opens the optional sleep survey.

  11. 39–44sPatient page: timeline and answers

    • Listening timeline: played, paused (red), skipped, where it stopped.
    • All answers, check-in by check-in. Skipped answers show “Skipped”, never 0.

    Each patient’s page shows every answer and the listening timeline: where they played, paused, skipped and stopped.

  12. 44–47sPress Export CSV

    • Participant dataset: one row per patient, ready for analysis.
    • Emails are never in the exports.

    Finally, Export CSV downloads the study data, one row per patient, ready for analysis.
